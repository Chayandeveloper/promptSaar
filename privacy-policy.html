Privacy Policy for Prompt Saar

📅 Effective Date: September 29, 2026 • 🔄 Last Updated: September 29, 2026

Fillosoft Technologies Private Limited ("we," "our," or "us") operates the Prompt Saar mobile application (the "App"). We are committed to protecting and respecting your privacy.

This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use Prompt Saar. Because our App does not require account creation and does not directly collect or store your personal data, your privacy is protected by design.

No Account Required

1 Information We Do Not Collect

Prompt Saar is designed to be fully usable without registering or providing personal information:

  • No Account Creation You are not required to sign up, log in, or create a profile to use Prompt Saar.
  • No Direct Data Collection We do not collect, store, or process personal identifying information such as your name, email address, phone number, physical address, or contact lists.

2 Third-Party Services and Automatically Collected Data

Although we do not directly collect personal data, Prompt Saar incorporates trusted third-party services for analytics and advertising purposes. These third-party tools may collect technical and usage data automatically in accordance with their respective privacy policies.

A. Analytics (Google Analytics for Firebase)

We use Google Analytics (or Firebase Analytics) to understand how users interact with the App, monitor performance, and improve user experience. The data collected may include:

  • Device model, operating system version, and unique device identifiers.
  • App usage metrics (e.g., screens viewed, feature interactions, app sessions).
  • Network state and crash reports.

B. Advertisements (Google AdMob)

Prompt Saar displays advertisements provided by third-party ad networks (such as Google AdMob). These ad providers may use cookies, device identifiers (e.g., Android Advertising ID or IDFA), or similar tracking technologies to serve relevant ads based on your app activity and generalized location.

To learn more about how Google handles user data in analytics and advertising, visit:

Google Privacy & Terms ↗ How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services ↗

3 AI Features & External Services

Prompt Saar provides prompt generation and discovery tools directly within the client app. Prompt Saar does not send your personal data or custom inputs to third-party artificial intelligence API services.

4 Children's Privacy

Prompt Saar is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or 16 in certain jurisdictions). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe that a child has provided us with personal information through third-party services, please contact us immediately, and we will take appropriate steps to remove such information.

5 Data Retention & User Rights

Because Prompt Saar does not maintain user accounts or store personal data on external servers, there is no personal data held by us to retain, modify, or delete.

Managing Ad Identifiers

You can manage or reset your device's advertising ID or opt out of personalized advertising directly through your device settings:

  • Android
    Settings > Google > Ads > Reset advertising ID or Opt out of Ads Personalization
  • iOS
    Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking
